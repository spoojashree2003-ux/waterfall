Safety & Visitor Information

Safety and environmental preservation are at the heart of our sanctuary. Please adhere to these guidelines.

⚠️
Emergency Assistance Always On Standby

Forest Rangers and First-Aid medical personnel patrol all 3 trails continuously. Emergency Call Posts are located every 400m.

🦤

Follow Marked Walking Paths

Always stay on defined timber boardwalks and stone steps to avoid hazardous slips and prevent soil erosion.

💧

Carry Enough Drinking Water

Keep hydrated by utilizing our reusable water-carrying bags and spring refill stations along the path.

👟

Wear Comfortable Walking Shoes

Sturdy hiking boots, running shoes, or rubber-soled footwear with good grip are strictly recommended.

⚠️

Follow Safety Signs

Pay close attention to trail depth signs, slippery rock warnings, and current water level notifications.

🗑️

Do Not Litter

We operate a strict Zero-Waste policy. Carry back all wrappers or place them in bio-bins at resting areas.

🚫

Stay Within Designated Areas

Do not jump across safety barriers or attempt climbing wet canyon cliffs around the waterfall cascade.

🧍

Follow Guide Instructions

Our local forest guides understand wildlife movements and stream depths. Please follow their advice.

👶

Keep Children Supervised

Extra care near stream edges, elevated bridges, and rocky view platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the trail.

Is the trek suitable for senior citizens and young children?
Yes — our Easy Trail features continuous shaded rest points specifically designed for elders and families with young children.
How does the water-carrying bag system work?
Reusable eco water bags are available free of charge at selected food spots when you refill spring water, letting you carry water hands-free along the trail.
Can visitors swim in the waterfall plunge pool?
Swimming is permitted in designated safe zones only, where life buoys, ropes, and certified lifeguards are stationed permanently.
